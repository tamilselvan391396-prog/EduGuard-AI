Important privacy & storage details

Photos are stored locally in this website's IndexedDB storage in Chrome on this phone, not uploaded to a cloud service and not automatically added to the normal DCIM/Gallery folder. Use Export photo backup to download copies.

The passkey is an app-level gallery access gate. Browser storage is not separately encrypted by this prototype; someone with technical access to browser data may bypass the page lock. Clearing Chrome site data can erase saved photos. Get school approval and inform students/parents as required before recording.

Prototype limitations: speech recognition depends on Android Chrome/browser support and may miss or misclassify sounds. Shouting is estimated using microphone loudness after speech is recognised; loud non-speech sounds can cause false triggers. This is not a certified classroom surveillance or emergency system.
